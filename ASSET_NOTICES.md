# Asset provenance and third-party notices

## Project-generated assets

The application icon, notification bell, and Ukrainian/English voice clips were generated with AI tools at the project owner's direction and are included with the owner's permission for use and redistribution with BDO Sea Assistant.

Relevant files:

- `assets/app_icon.ico`
- `assets/voice/notification_bell.wav`
- `assets/voice/uk/*.wav`
- `assets/voice/en/*.wav`

The manifest text and hashes describe the shipped audio. No third-party voice recording was used as source material.
